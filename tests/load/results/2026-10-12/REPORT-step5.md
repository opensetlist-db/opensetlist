# n14 qualification step 5 — cold admission (2026-10-12)

Design agreed with Codex (2 rounds, wiki Decisions log 2026-10-12 "Codex discussion on step 5"); tooling in PR #587 (Codex code review 3 cycles). ## Findings

1. **The first save during admission can reach no client by broadcast.** A0: save a1 at T0 + 5 s, 258 SDK clients already SUBSCRIBED (up to 5 s), server commit inside the PUT window (acquire 5 ms, exec 103 ms) — raw broadcast receipts **0 / 470**; every client learned the save from its 20 s ± 4 s periodic poll (SDK convergence p95 16.3 s, pages wave 0 15.8 s); the next saves at +20 s / +45 s reached 470 / 470. A2 (70 rows): a1 at +3 s again **0 / 490**. A0b and A4 / A4b: a1 reached 207 / 259 / 255 of 470 (a2 at +6 s: 312 / 363 / 414). The receiving subset in A0b/A4/A4b and the zero in A0/A2 do not line up with join age alone (A0b's receivers were 0–3 s old); the Realtime tenant had had no client of ours for ≈ 40 min before A0 and ≈ 14 min before A2, 3 min before A0b, 7 / 5 min before A4 / A4b. The harness's third exposure count (103 / 104 / 72 at a1 in A2 / A4 / A4b, column "dropped") is **not** channel churn: those are clients whose first SUBSCRIBED fell inside the 1.6–2.4 s PUT window (checked on A2's records: 0 disconnect events during a1); they are neither "connected through the save" nor "not yet", and are reported apart. The I-cycles (20 + 5 clients, no join storm, idle ≥ 30 min, observer as second vantage point) discriminate: idle tenant and fresh join both deliver the first broadcast to every client within 11–56 ms (I1b, I2a, I2b exercised and PASS; I1a's raw data identical). **The loss is a property of the 500-client admission burst** (~50 channel joins/s + 30 page joins), not of idleness or of a client's age. Show-day consequence: the first save after doors open while the audience is still joining may reach nobody by broadcast and repairs through the 20 s ± 4 s poll (the 3 s target is missed for that one save; 0 missing); a save made before the join storm (the T−10 check) and saves after the join settles (≥ 20 s in these runs) are delivered normally. Open with Supabase support (timestamps in the I-cycle conclusion).
2. **The first page loads after a fresh deployment take 3–6 s** (wave 0 = the first 5 pages, launched at T0 + 0.3 s): TTFB 0.2–0.3 s but DOMContentLoaded / complete setlist 3.0–4.5 s at 23 rows and 5.3–6.2 s at 70 rows; every later wave 0.5–1.2 s (70 rows ≤ 2 s). The SSR HTML is fast and complete (live seed present); the time goes to the new deployment's client assets being fetched cold through the CDN and hydrated. Show-day consequence: **never deploy within the last minutes before doors**; the T−10 check should include a couple of page loads on the production deployment so the edge is warm (runbook).
3. **The first write on a fresh deployment is slow**: PUT 1.8–2.4 s (A2 2,087 ms with acquire 310 ms; A4 1,822 / 794 ms acquire; A4b 2,427 / 528 ms) at 70 rows, 552 ms at 23 rows — a cold function instance + first pool connection + a 2.5× larger validation/snapshot. The PUT gate (p95 ≤ 1.5 s) fails on that single save in the 70-row runs; later saves 420–1,000 ms. Runbook: the T−10 check save warms the writer path too.
4. **Cold admission capacity is fine at 500**: 90–139 instances build the generic snapshot on arrival (657–909 builds in the admission minute), Σ peak 158–190 of the 400-client cap, EMAXCONN 0, 0 × 503, SDK first snapshot p95 2.3–2.8 s (23 → 70 rows), SSR p95 1.2 s under k6's 10/s arrival stream. Warming does not help arrivals (initial / catch-up reads bypass warm entries by design) and the numbers say it does not need to.
5. **70 rows (synthetic Fes bound)**: warm codec payload ko 202 / en 199 / ja 168 KB (20 % of `WARM_MAX_BYTES`), API JSON ko ≈ 177 KB, wire (br) ≈ 6 KB; steady saves converge SDK p95 1.8–3.2 s per save (23 rows: 1.4–1.5 s) — within the 3 s gate overall but with one save at 3.2 s; pages 1.3–2.0 s. With **warm lookups returning null** (A4b) the admission saves converge SDK p95 4.9 s (5.7 s for the save at +6 s) and the first save after admission 3.3 s: the degraded mode misses the 3 s gate at 70 rows under admission load. Statement for the show: at the Fes-size setlist the warm cache is required for the 3 s target; its unavailability during admission degrades to ~5 s, with 0 missing and no errors.
6. **Fetch shapes** (A1): R2 / R1 / pre-R1 notification shapes all read the exact saved head (471 / 161 / 129 = 100 %), 0 errors; the generic arrival wave p95 2.1 s on the cold fleet (soft gate 1 s) is the arrival cost, not a correctness issue.
7. **Harness / ops lessons**: `vercel redeploy` drops deployment-level `-e` env (A4 → re-run as A4b with a deployed pair); the "event unchanged" gate flagged this run's own save (fixed); `vercel inspect` prints to stderr (operator scripts); one admin login per run (firewall rate limit).

**I2b** (idle 30 min; same procedure, connect 09:52:00Z): check save at 09:52:12Z (PUT 638 ms) → 20/20 incumbents (38 / 42 / 42 ms) + 5/5 observer (5 ms spread), wake check ok; fresh cohort at +600 s, save w1 at 10:02:19Z (PUT 1,050 ms) → 20/20 fresh (54 / 55 / 56 ms), 20/20 incumbents, 5/5 observer; +40 s save 40/40 + 5/5 (1 periodic apply among 40). **VALID + PASS.**

**Conclusion of the I-cycles.** Four independent idle cycles (30–33 min without any client of ours each), 5 first-after-idle saves and 4 first-after-fresh-join saves, 20 local + 5 observer clients each: **every eligible client received every broadcast, 11–56 ms after the PUT response, from two vantage points.** The open finding from the 2026-10-11 observer smoke ("first save ~6 s after a fresh join reached nobody") is **not** an idle-tenant or a join-readiness effect at 20–25 clients. What does lose the first broadcast is the **500-client admission burst** (A0 0/470 at +5 s with 258 SUBSCRIBED; A2 0/490 at +3 s; A0b / A4 / A4b partial 207–259 of 470 at +3 s, 312–414 at +6 s; every later save 470/470). That is a Supabase Realtime behaviour under ~50 channel joins/s — the question for Supabase support, with these timestamps (dev project, 2026-10-12 06:38:10Z, 07:00:28Z, 06:44:44Z, 07:14:35Z, 07:22:09Z UTC). The wake procedure (I2a, I2b) holds as written: a check save whose broadcast is verified at a connected listener, listeners kept connected, and the next save after a fresh cohort joins reaches both cohorts.

## Runbook additions

- Deploy the show build well before doors (the first page loads after a deployment take 3–6 s until the edge is warm). The T−10 check = (a) a revision-bumping check save on the Fes event, (b) its broadcast verified at a connected listener (the observer workflow or a phone kept on the event page), (c) that listener stays connected until doors, (d) two or three page loads of the event page on the production domain in each locale.
- If the check save's broadcast does not arrive: retry once; a second miss → reload/reconnect the listeners and escalate (Supabase Realtime status, "Broadcast From Database Replication Lag" report) before relying on live updates.
- Expect the first save made while the audience is still streaming in to be delivered by the 20 s poll, not the broadcast; prefer to make the first show-time save after the join settles (admission of 500 took ~10 s here) or accept the one-off ~20 s.
- Catalog edits during a show: step-4 runbook unchanged.
